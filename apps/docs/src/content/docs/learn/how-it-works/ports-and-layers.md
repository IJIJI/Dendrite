A language is vocabulary only. What a program reads and produces arrives as port layers that compose onto it: a host's contract, a capability, the document's own declarations. The first layer to declare a name keeps it; a later one taking it is a problem blamed on the later layer. A layer's policy says who may edit it, who feeds its values, and whether it is saved. This page will show the layers in the playground's own inputs pane.

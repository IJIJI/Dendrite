Text becomes tokens, tokens become a program, the analyser checks it and resolves its types, the layers around it say what it may read and produce, and the evaluator walks what survives on demand. This page will be that in one diagram and five short paragraphs, each saying what you can rely on - and it hands off to [How it works](../../how-it-works/the-chain/) for why, and in what order.

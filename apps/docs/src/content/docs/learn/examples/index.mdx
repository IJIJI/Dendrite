## A grade from a score

Operators, a condition, and two outputs that share their working.

Things worth noticing:

- **`$score >= 60{:den}` is an operator**, and operators are sugar. This one becomes
  `Not(LessThan($score, 60)){:den}`, which is why there is no `GreaterThanOrEqual` op to look
  up. See [Operators and ops](../writing/operators-and-ops/).
- **`adjusted{:den}` is read twice**, by `topMark{:den}` and by the `finalScore{:den}` output,
  and computed once. A binding is a name for a value, not a macro.
- **The diagnostics line stays quiet** until you break something. Delete the `60` and it tells
  you where, before anything runs.

## A roll-up over three lists

Lists, a lambda, and an input the lambda closes over.

<Live client:only="react" source={heightsExample.source} ports={heightsExample.ports} />

- **`Concat{:den}`, `Filter{:den}`, `Length{:den}` and `Average{:den}` are ordinary ops.** There
  is no list syntax and no loop; iteration is what these are for.
- **`height => height >= $threshold{:den}` is a lambda** and a real closure: it captures
  `$threshold{:den}` from around it. Change the threshold and `howManyTall{:den}` moves while
  `average{:den}` does not, because `average` never reads it.
- **The three lists are separate inputs.** One input holding a record with three fields would
  say the same thing, and will once a layer can declare its own struct types.

## Take one apart

Both programs are in the repository, and both are checked by a test on every build, so they
cannot drift from the language. Open either in the playground with the corner button, change
whatever you like, and the URL becomes a link to exactly what you made.
